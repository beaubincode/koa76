Case study, client website

A3 Healing at Home

A non-medical in-home care provider in Batavia and the Fox Valley needed a warm, clear website that families could trust and use easily on their phones.

  • Built around the blue and green of A3’s logo, with a warm yellow that lights up the word “comfort”.
  • A consultation request form right on the first screen, with a reminder not to share private medical details.
  • Tap-to-call buttons, including a “Call Us Today” button that stays on screen.
  • Pages for services, service areas, reviews and FAQs, so families get answers before they call.
  • Open Graph tags, so shared links show a proper preview.

How I use your details

When you book a consultation or ask for a site review, I receive what you type into the form: your name, email, business or project name, website, notes and preferred time.

I use it only to reply to you and arrange the call or review. I don’t sell it, share it for marketing or add you to a mailing list.

Requests are delivered to my inbox by Cloudflare’s email service, which processes the message only to deliver it. The forms may use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that you’re not a bot. To have your details deleted, email cullenlabeau@otherstudio.dev.

This site sets no tracking cookies. Its fonts load from Google Fonts and its animation library from jsDelivr, and both services see your IP address when they send those files.
